Research

Research Interests

Areas of game technology and computer science that I am interested in exploring through graduate study and research.

Game Development & Game Technology

I am deeply curious about the engineering behind modern game engines, how they manage real-time rendering, physics, asset streaming, and complex entity systems at scale. My interest lies in understanding the architecture that allows vast, dynamic worlds to run smoothly, and in pushing these technologies to enable new forms of interactive storytelling.

Game AI & Intelligent Interactive Systems

Beyond simple state machines, I am fascinated by AI that can reason, adapt, and surprise players. I want to explore techniques like reinforcement learning, planning, and player modeling to create non‑player characters that feel alive, and systems that tailor challenge and narrative to each individual without breaking immersion.

Human-Computer Interaction (HCI)

I see games as the ultimate testbed for HCI, where feedback, affordances, and cognitive load must be balanced perfectly to keep players in flow. My research interest lies in understanding how users perceive and interact with complex systems, and how we can design interfaces that make advanced AI and simulation tools intuitive for creators and players alike.

Procedural Content Generation

I am captivated by the idea of algorithms that can generate entire worlds, not just isolated puzzles, but coherent, objective‑driven open environments where every terrain feature, quest, and NPC has purpose. My research curiosity revolves around constraint‑aware generation that respects gameplay logic, narrative arcs, and player progression, making each playthrough unique yet meaningfully designed.

Educational & Serious Games

Games have an unparalleled ability to teach through doing. I am intrigued by how we can embed complex scientific or historical concepts into interactive systems that encourage experimentation and failure, transforming abstract ideas into tangible, memorable experiences. I aim to investigate design principles that maximize learning outcomes while preserving the intrinsic motivation of play.

AI-Assisted Game Development

The rise of generative AI opens new frontiers for creators, from procedural asset generation to intelligent design assistants. I want to explore how machine learning models can collaborate with human designers, accelerating workflows while preserving artistic control, and how we can build tools that understand game design intent to suggest rather than replace.

Interactive Learning Systems

I envision learning environments that adapt not just to a student’s knowledge level, but to their curiosity. My interest focuses on systems that guide discovery rather than deliver instruction, using branching narratives, dynamic feedback, and AI‑driven mentorship to foster deep understanding across STEM and humanities.

XR & Computational Game Architecture

Extended reality merges the digital and physical in ways that demand rethinking spatial computing, latency, and interaction paradigms. I am keen to research the systems‑level challenges of building large‑scale XR experiences, handling massive state synchronization, persistent virtual worlds, and novel input methods that feel seamless and immersive.

Procedural Word Puzzle · Algorithms

WordLock

Prototype / Algorithm Design

Core Concept

A procedural word puzzle built around simultaneously satisfying row and column word constraints across a grid, with every puzzle generated algorithmically from a dictionary rather than authored by hand.

Current Development

  • Algorithms for generating valid puzzle grids from a dictionary
  • Constraint-based word placement and validation logic
  • Dictionary preprocessing and word indexing by length
  • Candidate pruning and search strategies
  • Difficulty scaling via grid size and word length

Research Direction

Investigating constraint-satisfaction and search strategies for procedurally generating puzzles that are simultaneously solvable, well-formed, and appropriately difficult.
